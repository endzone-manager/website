🏈 Redzone Boss: The Gridiron Strategy Simulator
Redzone Boss is a deep-dive American Football management simulation built for the modern strategist. Designed as a native desktop experience via Electron, it bridges the gap between high-level franchise administration and real-time tactical execution.

In Redzone Boss, you don't just watch the game; you own the organization. From the high-pressure environment of the Draft room to the split-second decisions on 4th & Goal, every choice defines your legacy.

🎯 The Vision
The project was born out of a desire for a "Manager-style" experience specifically for American Football—inspired by the addictive simplicity and depth of classics like Brasfoot or Elifoot, but evolved for the complexity of the gridiron.

Our goal is to provide a simulation where logic, statistics, and tactical intuition outweigh "arcade" reflexes.

🛠 Core Features
1. Real-Time Play Calling
Unlike static simulators, Redzone Boss puts you on the sidelines.

Call offensive and defensive plays in real-time.

React to your opponent's tendencies.

Manage the clock and timeouts during high-stakes drives.

2. Franchise Architecture
Take the helm as the General Manager:

Roster Construction: Balance your budget, manage the salary cap, and sign free agents.

Dynamic Scouting: Invest in scouting to find "diamonds in the rough" for the upcoming Draft.

Player Progression: Watch your rookies develop into All-Pros based on their performance and coaching staff.

3. Native Desktop Performance
By leveraging Electron, Redzone Boss offers:

A sleek, distraction-free UI.

Fast simulation processing for multi-season career modes.

Offline playability, focusing on the data-rich experience managers crave.

💻 Tech Stack
Framework: Electron (Desktop Integration)

Frontend: React / Tailwind CSS (Modern, Responsive Dashboard UI)

Engine: Custom-built simulation logic focused on probability, player attributes, and tactical modifiers.
